How these numbers are calculated

Definitions and grouping rules in plain language.

1

Turnover

Income recorded under G/L accounts beginning with 4. SAP stores revenue as negative, so the sign is reversed for display.

Displayed as money earned
2

Net expenses

Costs recorded under G/L accounts beginning with 5, including reversals and adjustments.

Debits and credits are shown as one net amount
3

Net profit

The amount left after subtracting all net expenses from turnover.
